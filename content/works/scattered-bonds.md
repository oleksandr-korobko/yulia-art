Scattered Bonds addresses the fractures produced by war through a suspended coral necklace, broken and partially dispersed across a transparent platform. Once associated with femininity, lineage, and Ukrainian cultural tradition, the necklace appears interrupted and incomplete, its missing beads registering absence as a material condition.

Suspended between collapse and suspension, the work reflects how identity and belonging are strained under displacement and loss. Rather than restoration, the sculpture holds the moment of rupture itself, asking what remains when cultural and familial bonds are irreversibly scattered.
